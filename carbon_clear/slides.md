# CO₂ Science Overview

CO₂, or carbon dioxide, is a naturally occurring gas vital to life, but excess in the atmosphere disrupts Earth’s balance.

- **Greenhouse Effect**: CO₂ traps heat, keeping our planet warm.
- **Rising Levels**: Human activities have boosted CO₂ to levels unprecedented in 800,000+ years.
- **Global Warming**: More CO₂ = higher global temperatures.

# Causes of CO₂ Emissions

- **Fossil Fuels:** Burning coal, oil, gasoline for energy and transport.
- **Deforestation:** Fewer trees = less CO₂ absorbed from air.
- **Industry:** Cement, steel, and chemical production.
- **Agriculture:** Soil disturbance and livestock.

# Effects on Climate

- **Temperature Rise:** Global average temp up ~1.2°C since 1880.
- **Weather Extremes:** More floods, droughts, heatwaves, wildfires.
- **Melting Ice & Sea Rise:** Glacial melt, rising sea levels threaten coasts.
- **Ecosystem Shifts:** Habitats change, some species at risk.

## Key Takeaways – Effects on Climate

- Warming intensifies natural disasters.
- Coastal and island populations face greatest risk.
- Biodiversity loss threatens harmony of life.

# Solutions and Actions

- **Clean Energy:** Solar, wind, hydro, nuclear.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Better buildings, appliances.
- **Sustainable Transport:** Electric vehicles, cycling, public transit.
- **Conservation:** Protect forests & ecosystems.
- **Policy:** Carbon pricing, emission regulations.
- **Personal Action:** Lower, reuse, recycle, choose plant-rich diets.
